# Olivia KB — Squads & Programs (mined from Intercom member questions)

Source: MDS Intercom support history (help.milliondollarsellers.com). Only genuine
member/prospect-initiated questions about how Squads & Programs work are included.
Staff email, outbound newsletters, WildApricot admin-copies, event-registration
confirmations, and pure billing/app-bug threads were filtered out. All PII stripped;
quotes are ≤20 words.

**Scope note:** "Squads" is by far the dominant Programs topic in Intercom. "Mastermind"
threads are almost entirely *event* registrations (e.g. Bali Mastermind, AI Mastermind =
ticketed events), not the recurring-program concept — those belong to the events/ops bucket.
"Mentor / mentorship / cohort / accountability" surface almost no member questions of their own.

---

## 1. Squad cost / fees  (the #1 recurring question)

**Canonical question:** How much does a Squad cost? Is the placement fee the *total* cost,
or are there additional monthly/recurring charges on top?

- Came up: recurring — the single most-asked Squads question.
- Quote: *"is that all the costs or will there be additional monthly costs as well?"*
- **Team answer:** A Squad is a **one-time $1,500 placement fee** — billed as **2 × $750**
  (50% upfront, 50% once you're actually placed into a Squad). **No monthly/recurring
  payments** after that. The fee is separate from (and on top of) your normal MDS membership.

## 2. "FREE of charge" vs the placement fee — free-vs-paid confusion

**Canonical question:** The Squads page says the program is "FREE of charge," but also lists a
$1,500 placement fee and monthly membership prices — which is it? What am I actually paying for?

- Came up: paired with the cost question (same members raise both).
- Quote: *"I was a bit confused on this part, the part about FREE of charge"*
- **Team answer (clarification):** The Squad **program itself carries no recurring subscription**
  — that's what "free of charge" means: it's a member benefit with no monthly squad fee. The only
  Squad-specific charge is the **one-time $1,500 placement fee**. The separate monthly/annual prices
  shown are the underlying **MDS membership** tiers, not a Squad cost. So: one-time placement fee +
  your existing membership = no extra recurring squad cost.

## 3. How / where do I sign up for a Squad? (and "the link is broken")

**Canonical question:** How do I register for a Squad / where's the sign-up link? Several members
report the "learn more" or registration link from the Squads email doesn't work.

- Came up: a cluster of ~5 near-identical member replies to the Squads invitation email.
- Quote: *"interested but the link is invalid … I would like to sign up"*
- **Team answer:** Members sign up via the Squad registration/placement form; when the emailed link
  is broken the team resends a working registration link. (Signup is a discrete registration step,
  not automatic with membership.)

## 4. Did my sign-up go through, and when will I be placed? (placement timing / cohorts)

**Canonical question:** Can you confirm I'm signed up for a Squad? Is there a deadline, and when do
I actually get placed into a group?

- Came up: recurring around each registration window.
- Quote: *"can I do so today? I think it is the deadline today."*
- **Team answer:** Placement runs in **batches/cohorts**, not instantly. The team confirms your
  registration, then **assigns members to Squads in a following window** and follows up directly
  (e.g. "we'll be assigning members to their squads the following week"; a past cycle noted
  "Placement will be in July"). Registration opens for a period, then a placement round follows.

## 5. Pilot-team / moderator fee waivers

**Canonical question:** As a Squad pilot-team member (or moderator), shouldn't my placement fee be
waived / removed from my account?

- Came up: occasionally (role-based).
- Quote: *"as part of the pilot team of the squad, those fees should be waived"*
- **Team answer:** No full resolution on file — request is routed to the **Programs team**, who
  handle fee adjustments for pilot/moderator roles. (Confirms fees can be waived for certain roles;
  handled case-by-case by Programs, not self-serve.)

## 6. What is a Squad / what do I get? (interest & info requests)

**Canonical question:** What exactly is the Squad program and what's the benefit of joining?

- Came up: implicit in "interested, send me more info" replies to the Squads invitation.
- Quote: *"interested in more info"*
- **Team answer:** Squads are positioned as one of the **most impactful member benefits** — a
  small peer accountability group. (Members mainly ask to be pointed to the details / sign-up;
  no single canonical "what is a squad" explainer captured in these threads — candidate KB gap.)

## Notable KB gaps (questions members have that lack a clean canonical answer on file)
- A single plain-language "**What is a Squad and what do you get**" explainer.
- Clear public statement of **eligibility** (who can join) and **commitment/expectations**.
- Exact **timing/cadence** of registration windows and placement rounds (members repeatedly ask
  "is it open now / when's the deadline / when am I placed").
