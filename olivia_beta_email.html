What it is

The MDS AI Assistant lives on WhatsApp and answers questions using MDS's own knowledge. It searches across the Facebook group, the WhatsApp chats you're in, the events calendar, the member and partner directories, the video library, and your own membership information.

It doesn't search the internet. If the answer isn't in MDS, it'll tell you rather than guess.

What to expect

This is an early beta. Some answers will land well. Some will miss the mark. A few will simply be wrong. That's exactly why you're here.

You don't need to investigate bugs or write long reports. Just use it naturally. If something feels off, let us know — we'll take care of the rest. We read every conversation from the beta group to understand where the assistant struggles and what to improve next.

The easiest way to help

  • 👍 or 👎 any answer. That alone tells us a lot.
  • If something is clearly wrong and you have a moment, send us a screenshot. Entirely optional, but it helps us fix things faster.

What it can help with

Facebook — posts and the discussion under them: what people are talking about, who raised what, and links back to the original thread.

  • "What did I miss on Facebook this week?"
  • "What are people saying about the 75-character title change?"

Your WhatsApp chats — only the chats you're in. Catch up on conversations or ask what people have said about a topic.

  • "What did I miss in my chats this week?"
  • "Catch me up on MDS TikTok."

Members — find people by expertise, location or background.

  • "Who knows PPC?"
  • "Tell me about [member name]."

Events — what's coming up, where it's happening, who's going, and what you're already registered for.

  • "What events are coming up?"
  • "Any MDS meetups near Dallas?"

Partners and deals — trusted partners and the offers available through MDS.

  • "Any deals for 3PL?"
  • "Is there a partner for accounting?"

The video library — 1,000+ recorded sessions, searchable by topic, speaker or date, and it can send you the materials attached to a session.

  • "Is there a video about hiring a C-suite?"
  • "Send me the deck from [session name]."

Your membership — your plan, renewal date, next invoice and what you're registered for.

  • "When does my membership renew?"
  • "What's my next invoice?"

Working through a problem — it pulls from several sources at once: relevant discussions, experienced members, and vetted partners.

  • "My account got suspended. Who can help?"

These are only examples to give you a sense of what's possible. Don't feel limited by them — the best way to use the assistant is exactly as you would another MDS member.

When it can't do something itself

Changing your details, updating a registration, fixing a payment — it will offer to open a ticket with the MDS team. Reply YES and it's filed, and you get a ticket number back. The team typically replies within an hour on weekdays.

Current limitations

  • Facebook: about a year of history. Comments are complete for 2026; older posts may be missing their discussion threads.
  • WhatsApp: history currently starts in late April 2026.
  • Videos: the library is searchable, but recordings don't have transcripts yet. It can point you to the right session and send any attached materials, but it can't tell you what was said during the recording.
  • Restricted sessions: some recordings are limited to specific memberships or chapters. You'll see the title and speaker, but not the content unless you have access.
  • Member profiles: only as detailed as members have made them.

Privacy

Personal information stays private. The assistant won't share contact details or anyone's private business information, and it won't give opinions about whether someone can be trusted. Anything already visible in the member directory it will happily share.
