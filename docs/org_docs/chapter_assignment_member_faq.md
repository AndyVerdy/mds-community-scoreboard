Chapter assignment — member FAQ
Curated member-facing digest of the internal Chapter Assignment SOP (which stays staff-only).
Approved by Andy 2026-08-19 in chat ("fix"). Source of truth for edits: this file, in git.

How do I get assigned to a chapter?
Your chapter comes from your address when you join — every member gets the one chapter that
covers their region. If you have moved or think you are in the wrong chapter, the MDS team
handles changes — Mille can file the request for you.

Can I be in more than one chapter?
Yes — additional chapters are paid access: $1,200 USD per additional chapter per year. This is
intended for members located between two regions, with business operations in several locations,
or who travel often to another city or country. Requests go to chapter@mds.co.

Is any chapter non-geographic?
The MDS Women's Chapter is global and open to all female members.
